FAMTREE — MASTER SPECIFICATION
September 2026


================================================================================
FAMTREE — PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
================================================================================

1. Product Overview
FamTree is a web-based digital family-tree system. The first production scope is restricted to one preconfigured family tree belonging to the user's family/surname. The product is designed to let family members register, securely link an account to an existing family-tree member, view the tree, and view selected member information.

2. Product Goal
Create a simple, structured, family-only digital tree that can later expand into a broader family platform without overloading users during registration or the first release.

3. Version 1 Scope
• Web application, hosted online.
• Family tree is preconfigured.
• Every account must be linked to one tree member.
• A tree member may exist without an account.
• Registration is intentionally lightweight and orderly rather than presenting every question at once.
• Initial registration fields: user's name, father's name, mother's name, surname, email, phone number, password, and re-enter password.
• Date of birth is not requested during registration.
• Email and phone verification are part of the registration flow.
• Under-15 users cannot create accounts.
• For the agreed initial age rule: users above 15 and below 50 require phone and email; for 50+ the email is optional and phone remains required.
• A second email is optional.
• If both registered email and phone are inaccessible, recovery goes to Contact Admin.
• Successful registration checks the preconfigured family tree and links the account to the appropriate member.
• If a matching member is found, the user is shown identifying details for confirmation, including birth date and family-related identifying information.
• If duplicate names exist, multiple candidates are shown with name, father's name, mother's name, and date of birth.
• If the details do not match or no member is found, the user is sent to Contact Admin.
• Contact Admin page contains a short instruction, a WhatsApp option, and an email option. WhatsApp opens the chat without prefilled text.
• Returning users who log in are taken to the main dashboard.
• Family Tree opens centered on the logged-in member.
• Initial tree view shows the logged-in member, parents, spouse if any, siblings if any, and children if any. Users can zoom out and pan to explore.
• Member cards use a common pop-up format for the logged-in member and other members.
• Pop-up information includes only information not already obvious from the tree: name, birth date, active/inactive/deceased status, contact information when shared, and optional study/work information. Work/study details are title/status based; specific details are shown only when the member chooses to provide/share them.
• Personal profile edits may be allowed for editable contact/status/work-study information. Name, parents' names, and date of birth are protected and require admin handling.
• Changing email or phone requires verification.
• Level-2 admin has broad edit/delete rights subject to preserving tree integrity.
• Later releases may introduce the age-based 30+ linking restriction; it is explicitly not part of the initial release.
• No account is created for a person who is not represented in the preconfigured family tree; they are directed to Contact Admin instead.

4. Authentication
Login uses the person's name components in this order: user's name, father's name, mother's name, surname, followed by password. Registration also includes password and re-enter password. Account recovery uses the normal recovery route; the system should not add lengthy family-question recovery in version 1.

5. Non-Goals for Version 1
No public multi-family onboarding, no complex age-based approval workflow, no long registration questionnaire, no date-of-birth collection at registration, and no complicated recovery questionnaire.

6. Roadmap
Development is organized as stages rather than artificial feature releases. Stage 1–20 constitute the Alpha milestone. The next 25 stages constitute the Beta milestone. Further stages lead to the main/public production version. Alpha is intended for the project owner; Beta is intended for controlled testing (the working plan records 15 testers); V1.0 is the public production release.

7. Success Criteria
A family member can register with minimal friction, verify contact information, correctly identify/link their tree member, log in later, reach the dashboard, open a useful centered family-tree view, and view consistent member pop-ups without exposing information the member has not chosen to share.

FAMTREE — FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (FRD)
================================================================================

FR-01 Registration
The system shall provide fields for name, father's name, mother's name, surname, email, phone number, password, and re-enter password.

FR-02 Registration sequence
The system shall present registration in an orderly, staged manner rather than asking every possible family detail at once.

FR-03 Age rule
The system shall prevent account creation for users under 15. The initial agreed contact-verification rule is: above 15 and below 50 requires phone and email; age 50+ may use phone with email optional.

FR-04 Email/phone verification
The system shall verify required contact channels before account completion. A second email is optional.

FR-05 Family membership check
The system shall check the preconfigured family tree before completing account linking.

FR-06 Single unique match
When a unique family member match is found, the system shall display identifying details and ask the user to confirm that the profile is theirs.

FR-07 Duplicate match
When multiple members have the same name, the system shall display separate choices using name, father's name, mother's name, and date of birth.

FR-08 Mismatch/no match
When the person cannot establish the correct match, the system shall route them to Contact Admin.

FR-09 Contact Admin
The page shall provide a short instruction plus WhatsApp and email contact methods. WhatsApp shall open the admin chat without prefilled text.

FR-10 Login
Login shall accept the name components in the order: name, father's name, mother's name, surname, plus password.

FR-11 Recovery
Forgot-password/recovery shall use the registered recovery channels. If both email and phone are inaccessible, Contact Admin is the final fallback.

FR-12 Dashboard
Successful login shall take the user to the main dashboard.

FR-13 Family tree view
The tree shall open centered on the logged-in member and initially show immediate family: parents, spouse if any, siblings if any, and children if any.

FR-14 Tree navigation
The user shall be able to zoom out and pan to explore other family members.

FR-15 Member pop-up
All member cards shall use a consistent pop-up format.

FR-16 Pop-up content
The pop-up shall avoid repeating relationship information already obvious from the tree and show name, birth date, life/status state, shared contact information, and optional study/work information.

FR-17 Privacy of optional information
Optional work/study/contact details shall not be shown when the member has not supplied/chosen to share them.

FR-18 Profile edits
Users may edit permitted personal/contact/status/work-study information. Protected identity/tree-structure fields such as name, parents' names, and date of birth require admin handling.

FR-19 Re-verification
Email or phone changes shall trigger verification.

FR-20 Admin
Level-2 admin may broadly edit/delete tree information provided tree integrity is maintained.

FR-21 Member without account
A tree member may exist without an account.

FR-22 Account-to-member rule
Every account must ultimately be linked to a tree member.

FAMTREE — TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (TRD)
================================================================================

1. Architecture
Web application architecture: React + TypeScript + Vite frontend; Supabase backend using PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, Realtime, and Edge Functions. Public internet deployment is planned.

2. Frontend
React component architecture should separate authentication, dashboard, family-tree visualization, member pop-ups, profile management, and admin interfaces. TypeScript types should represent account, member, relationship, verification, and permission states.

3. Backend
PostgreSQL is the source of truth for family members and relationships. Supabase Auth manages credentials and sessions. Edge Functions should handle privileged server-side operations and verification-sensitive flows.

4. Data integrity
Family relationships must be modeled explicitly rather than inferred only from UI. Delete/update operations must preserve valid parent/child/spouse/sibling relationships and prevent orphaned or contradictory records.

5. Authorization
Role-based authorization shall distinguish normal members from administrative roles. Level-2 admin receives broad edit/delete capabilities subject to integrity rules. Database-level policies should enforce access rather than relying only on UI controls.

6. Privacy
Contact, study, work, and other optional profile information shall have explicit visibility rules. The UI must not display unset optional information.

7. Authentication security
Passwords must be handled by the authentication provider and never stored as plaintext. Verification codes/tokens must expire and be single-use. Sessions should be revocable.

8. Recovery
Use standard authenticated recovery mechanisms through verified email/phone where supported. Contact Admin is the final manual route when normal recovery cannot work.

9. Performance
The tree UI should initially load a focused neighborhood around the logged-in member rather than rendering an unnecessarily large tree view at full detail. Pan/zoom should not repeatedly reload unchanged member data.

10. Deployment
The first public-facing application is intended to be hosted online. Environment secrets must remain outside the frontend bundle.

FAMTREE — UI/UX SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. Design principle
Simple, family-oriented, low-friction, progressive disclosure. Registration should not feel like a long questionnaire.

2. Registration screens
Step 1: identity basics — name, father's name, mother's name, surname. Step 2: contact — email and phone according to the age rule. Step 3: password — password and re-enter password. Verification is then completed before account creation/linking.

3. Matching screen
Show the detected family member and a concise set of identifying details. Ask: 'Is this you?' If duplicate names exist, show candidate cards with name, father's name, mother's name, and date of birth.

4. Contact Admin screen
Short message explaining that an issue occurred and instructing the user to contact the admin. Two clear actions: WhatsApp and Email. WhatsApp opens the chat with no prefilled message.

5. Login screen
Name, father's name, mother's name, surname, password, and a clear recovery action.

6. Dashboard
The main landing screen after successful login. The Family Tree is a primary action.

7. Family Tree
Start centered on the logged-in member. Show parents, spouse if any, siblings if any, and children if any. Keep the initial zoom close; allow zoom-out and pan.

8. Member pop-up
Consistent layout for every member. Do not duplicate obvious tree relationships. Show name, birth date, active/inactive/deceased status, shared contact details, and optional work/study information.

9. Profile editing
Clearly separate editable personal details from protected family identity/tree-structure details.

FAMTREE — DATA MODEL & DATABASE SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

Core entities
User account, Family Member, Family Relationship, Verification Record, Role/Permission, Contact/Visibility Preference, Admin Support Contact.

Family Member
Suggested logical fields: member_id, name, father's_member_id, mother's_member_id, surname, date_of_birth, life_status, phone, primary_email, secondary_email_optional, study_status_optional, work_status_optional, work_details_optional, profile_visibility settings, created_at, updated_at.

Account
Suggested logical fields: account_id, auth_user_id, linked_member_id, verification state, role, created_at, updated_at.

Relationship
Suggested fields: relationship_id, source_member_id, target_member_id, relationship_type, created_at. Relationship types must be constrained to supported family relationships.

Integrity constraints
Every account has exactly one linked member. A member can have no account. Protected identity fields should not be changed by ordinary member edits. Relationship changes must be validated.

Visibility
Optional contact/work/study fields require explicit visibility handling so absent or private values do not appear in member pop-ups.

Note
This is a logical schema. Exact SQL names, indexes, foreign keys, RLS policies, and migration files should be finalized during implementation without changing the agreed product behavior.

FAMTREE — API SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

Authentication
Register, verify email, verify phone, login, logout, password reset, session refresh/revocation.

Family matching
Resolve a registration identity against the preconfigured family tree; return one candidate or multiple candidates with safe identifying fields.

Member
Get member profile, update permitted profile fields, request protected-field change through admin workflow.

Tree
Get the logged-in member's immediate family neighborhood and request expanded tree data for zoom-out/navigation.

Admin
Manage members and relationships according to role; validate tree integrity before mutations.

Support
Return configured admin contact channels used by the Contact Admin page.

Security contract
The API must never return hidden/private profile fields merely because a client asks for them. Authorization must be enforced server-side.

FAMTREE — AUTHENTICATION, SECURITY & PRIVACY SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

Authentication rules
Under 15: no account. Above 15 and below 50: phone and email required. Age 50+: phone required, email optional. Second email is optional.

Verification
Required contact channels must be verified. Any email or phone change must be verified again.

Account recovery
Use normal recovery mechanisms. Do not add the previously discussed lengthy family-question recovery to version one. If both email and phone are inaccessible, route to Contact Admin.

Impersonation protection
Family-member matching is not itself a substitute for credential security. Existing linked accounts should be accessed through normal login/recovery. The initial version should avoid exposing sensitive data during matching.

Privacy
Only profile information the member has supplied and chosen to share should be displayed in optional sections. Contact and employment/study details should be minimized.

Admin security
Administrative operations must be protected server-side and audited. Level-2 admin has broad edit/delete capability but cannot be allowed to corrupt family-tree integrity.

Later rule
The age-based 30+ admin approval restriction for profile linking is a later-version feature, not an Alpha/Beta/V1.0 initial feature.

FAMTREE — TEST PLAN
================================================================================

Registration
Test under-15 rejection; 15–49 contact requirements; 50+ email optional; password confirmation; email/phone verification; optional second email.

Family matching
Test unique match, duplicate-name match, incorrect candidate, no match, and Contact Admin routing.

Login/recovery
Test valid login, invalid credentials, password recovery, loss of one recovery channel, loss of both channels and Contact Admin fallback.

Tree
Test centering on logged-in member; parents/spouse/siblings/children rendering; zoom-out; pan; member pop-up consistency.

Profile
Test permitted edits, protected fields, verification on contact changes, optional-field hiding.

Admin
Test level-2 admin edits/deletes and integrity constraints.

Security
Test authorization boundaries, session expiry/revocation, hidden-field protection, password handling, and privilege escalation attempts.

Acceptance
Alpha: owner-only validation. Beta: controlled testing with the planned 15 testers. Main/V1.0: production acceptance against the agreed requirements.

FAMTREE — STAGE-BASED DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP
================================================================================

Stage philosophy
The project will be developed as a sequence of stages. A milestone is reached only after the required stage set is completed.

Stages 1–20 — Alpha
Complete the foundational product through 20 stages. Alpha is for the project owner and is the first coherent working version.

Stages 21–45 — Beta
Complete the next 25 stages. Beta is for controlled testing; the current plan uses 15 testers.

After Stage 45 — Main/Public
Continue through the remaining production-readiness stages until the main public version/V1.0 is ready.

Suggested stage groups
Architecture → authentication → registration → verification → family matching → tree model → tree UI → member pop-ups → profile editing → admin tools → security → testing → deployment → beta fixes → production hardening.

Important
The stage plan is intentionally separate from feature scope. New features should be assigned to stages rather than creating unnecessary version labels.

================================================================================
FAMTREE — ROLES & PERMISSIONS
================================================================================

Normal member
Can log in, view the family tree, view member pop-ups, and edit only the profile fields permitted by the product rules.

Level-2 admin
Can broadly edit/delete members and relationships across the tree, subject to tree-integrity validation. This role is not restricted to only its own profile.

Protected fields
Name, parents' names, and date of birth are not ordinary self-service edits. Changes require an admin-controlled process.

Later roles/rules
The later 30+ linking restriction is intentionally excluded from the initial release and may be introduced in a future stage.
